VolleyBallR

Interactive charts of every volleyball attack. Pick a player or team, filter by how the play developed, and see on the court where attacks land and how they turn out.

Used by the University of Utah women's volleyball team for about ten years.

View the source on GitHub

The story

VolleyBallR was one of our very first projects. In November 2016 we started from BallR, Todd W. Schneider's open-source app for interactive NBA shot charts, and rebuilt it for volleyball.

That meant redrawing the court as a volleyball half court, replacing the NBA stats feed with volleyball scouting data, and adding the filters a volleyball staff actually uses: attack combination, set call, pass rating, blockers, rotation and more.

The University of Utah women's volleyball team went on to use it in production for about ten years. When new seasons brought new data, the app was updated to keep up, including a 2021 change so it could read the 2020 season's files.

How it works

Volleyball court charts

Attacks are plotted on a redrawn volleyball half court, with the attack line marked, instead of a basketball court.

Player, team or opponent

Chart a single player, a whole team, or what an opponent has done against one team or the entire league.

Volleyball filters

Narrow attacks down by attack combination, result, pass, dig or free-ball rating, pace, number of blockers, rotation, set rating, set call, set zone and attack speed.

In or out of system

Compare attacks run in system with those run out of system.

Three chart types

Switch between hexagon, scatter and heat map views of the same attacks.

Scouting data

Loads attack data exported from match scouting, with court coordinates and result codes for each attack.

Built with

Written in R as a Shiny web app, with charts drawn using ggplot2 and hexbin.
