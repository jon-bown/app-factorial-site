How it works

Choose the person

Pick the politician whose gaffes and quotes you want to hear.

Choose the sound

Choose the sound you want to play by tapping once, tapping on another sound will stop the previous and start the next one. Hold down on the sound to rearrange the cells to put your favorites toward the top!

Open the sound editor

Tap the sound wave button in the top right corner to open the sound editor. Change the pitch and the speed of the sounds you play and have a laugh.
