Android app

Watchlist

Build custom lists of the movies and TV shows you want to watch, check them off as you go, and see where each one is available to stream, rent or buy.

View the source on GitHub

How it works

Explore

Browse popular, now playing, trending and upcoming titles, and switch between movies and TV with one tap.

Your lists

Create as many watchlists as you like, and add a title to several of them at once.

Seen it

Check off what you've watched, then filter each list by all, seen or unseen. Swipe left to remove a title or a whole list.

Where to watch

See which services have a title to stream, rent or buy in your region.

Search

Find any movie or TV show and add it straight to your lists.

Your language, your region

Pick a language for movie info and a region for streaming availability. Both default to your device settings.

Built with

Written in Kotlin. Accounts and lists are stored in Firebase, and movie and TV data comes from The Movie Database (TMDB).

  • Kotlin
  • Android
  • Firebase Auth
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Retrofit
  • OkHttp
  • Glide
  • TMDB API
